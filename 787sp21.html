Algebraic Curves

This course is a follow-up to last semester's course on Commutative Algebra. See the typed lecture notes.

This semester we will cover "one dimensional mathematics." This is based on the amazing 19th century synthesis of three seemingly different subjects:

- Algebraic Curves: Sets in the plane defined by a single polynomial equation f(x,y)=0.
- Riemann Surfaces: One dimensional compact complex manifolds.
- Function Fields: Field extensions over the complex numbers of transcendence degree one.
