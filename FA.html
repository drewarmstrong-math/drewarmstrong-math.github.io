Fancy Algebra

In Fall 2016 I taught a topics course called Fancy Algebra: A categorical introduction to representation theory at the Universitaet des Saarlandes. The goal of the course was to teach myself the categorical foundations necessary to understand things such as tensor categories. I enjoyed it so much that I gave another version of the course in Fall 2017 at the University of Miami. Only the first part of the course got typed up.
  • Part I: Adjoint Functors
  • Part II: Enriched Categories
  • Part III: Abelian Categories and Topoi
  • Part IV: Morita Theory and Tannaka Theory
